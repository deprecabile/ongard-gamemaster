# The Sentient Races of Ondgard

Ondgard is a multiracial world. All races are **purely biological** entities with real physical needs: hunger, thirst,
fatigue, disease, ageing. No race possesses innate magical powers — magic is a learned discipline, accessible (in
theory) to anyone with the ability and proximity to a Dimensional Rift.

After millennia of coexistence, societies are largely cosmopolitan. No "pure racial kingdoms" exist: a dwarf may be a
merchant in Porto Cristallo (Ostria) and a human may live in the forges of Thurigard. However, historical
concentrations and cultural affinities create non-uniform demographic distributions.

---

## Human (UMN)

- **Height:** 155–195 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Plains, hills, coasts.

Versatile and ambitious, humans are the most widespread and numerous race on Ondgard. They adapt to any environment and
climate, building civilisations in every corner of the planet. Their strength lies not in specialisation but in
flexibility: they excel as soldiers, merchants, farmers, scholars and politicians.

**Distribution:** Dominant in the Empire of Voth (almost entirely human), strongly present in all kingdoms.
They constitute the planetary demographic majority.

**Biological traits:** No extreme adaptations. Life expectancy: 60–80 years. Rapid maturation compared to elves, which
makes them prolific and demographically dominant.

---

## Elf (ELF)

- **Height:** 170–210 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Forests, woodlands, clearings.

Graceful and long-lived, elves possess keen senses and a natural predisposition for the arts, knowledge and diplomacy.
Their longevity makes them patient and reflective, yet slow to adapt to change.

**Distribution:** Strong presence in the League of Ostria, where their cultural inclination pairs well with the
mercantile and academic world. Communities scattered across all kingdoms, often in roles as advisors, archivists or
ambassadors.

**Biological traits:** Life expectancy: 300–400 years. Vision and hearing superior to humans. Slower metabolism, lower
endurance under prolonged physical effort. Low fertility — an elven couple produces 1–2 children in a lifetime.

---

## Dwarf (NAN)

- **Height:** 110–140 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Mountains, caves, underground.

Sturdy and tenacious, dwarves are unrivalled artisans and miners. Their culture is founded on mastery of craft, clan
loyalty and a collective memory that forgets neither wrongs nor debts. "A dwarf always pays, for good or ill" is a
common saying.

**Distribution:** Concentrated in the Kingdom of Thurim, especially in the metal-rich central mountain regions. Their
forges supply armies and merchants across the entire eastern continent.

**Biological traits:** Life expectancy: 150–200 years. Dense bones, compact musculature, exceptional resistance to
fatigue and cold. Vision adapted to dim light. Less agile than humans in open spaces.

---

## Gnome (GNO)

- **Height:** 80–110 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Hills, woodlands, meadows.

Curious and inventive, gnomes compensate for their small stature with unrivalled mechanical and optical ingenuity. They
are the finest precision artisans on the planet: chronometers, lenses, navigational mechanisms and scientific
instruments are almost all of gnomish make.

**Distribution:** Concentrated in the League of Ostria, particularly on the island of Gnomeria (capital Meccanopoli).
Ostria's naval monopoly rests in part on gnomish technology: nautical charts, tidal chronometers for Shinosa's tides
and optical instruments.

**Biological traits:** Life expectancy: 80–120 years. Slender fingers and exceptional close-range vision (ideal for
precision work). Mediocre distance vision. Rapid metabolism — they eat little but often.

---

## Orc (ORC)

- **Height:** 175–220 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Steppes, marshes, wastelands.

Strong and proud, orcs follow a rigid tribal honour code that governs every aspect of life: from combat to the division
of food. Their physical endurance is legendary — they survive in environments that would break other races.

**Distribution:** Dominant in the Dominion of Thia (orcish and goblin khanate), present in significant numbers also in
the Kingdom of Thurim, where coexistence with humans and dwarves is pragmatic and martial. In northern Thurim, orcs and
humans fight side by side against common threats.

**Biological traits:** Life expectancy: 50–70 years. Powerful musculature, thick and resilient skin, high pain
threshold. Slightly faster healing than humans (not magical — pure biology). High fertility, early maturation.

---

## Troll (TRO)

- **Height:** 200–280 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Marshes, caves, dark forests.

Imposing and ancient, trolls are creatures bound to the earth in an almost symbiotic manner. Their skin is thick and
leathery, covered in growths that resemble bark or lichen. They are slow of thought but not stupid — their intelligence
is concrete and practical, oriented toward survival.

**Distribution:** Rare and solitary. Found at the margins of civilisation, in the wild areas of every continent. Some
live in primitive communities in deep marshes or the oldest forests. In cities they are an uncommon sight, often
employed as porters, guards or heavy labourers.

**Biological traits:** Life expectancy: 100–150 years. Accelerated tissue regeneration (superficial wounds heal in days
rather than weeks — not instantaneous, not magical). Skin resistant to light cuts. Slow metabolism, needing little food.
Vulnerable to fire (the leathery skin burns easily and heals poorly).

---

## Goblin (GOB)

- **Height:** 90–130 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Caves, underground, ruins.

Cunning and numerous, goblins compensate for their small stature with shrewdness, ingenuity and impressive
prolificacy. They are masters of traps, ambushes and improvisation. Their reputation as thieves and swindlers is only
partially deserved — many goblins are skilled artisans, scouts or merchants.

**Distribution:** Present in large numbers in the Dominion of Thia alongside orcs, where they form the base of the
tribal structure. Goblin communities are found in the slums of almost every major city, often in underground quarters.

**Biological traits:** Life expectancy: 40–60 years. Excellent night vision, keen hearing. Agility and reflexes above
average. Physically fragile — bones break easily. Very high fertility (litters of 3–5), maturation in 8–10 years.

---

## Reptilian (RET)

- **Height:** 170–200 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Marshes, jungles, deserts.

Cold-blooded and calculating, the reptilians are among the most ancient races of Ondgard. Their society is based on
rigid hierarchies and ruthless logic. They are not cruel by nature, but their lack of facial expression and pragmatic
behaviour makes them unsettling to other races.

**Distribution:** Concentrated in equatorial and subtropical regions, where the warm climate supports their ectothermic
metabolism. Significant communities in the southern jungles of the Empire of Voth and in coastal marshes. Rare in cold
regions.

**Biological traits:** Life expectancy: 80–120 years. Ectothermy (cold-blooded) — dependent on ambient heat, lethargic
below 10 °C. Scaly skin resistant to abrasion and parasites. Limited limb regeneration (fingers can regrow, not entire
limbs). Exceptional sense of smell (forked tongue). Efficient metabolism — they can fast for weeks.

---

## Xeroba (XER)

- **Height:** 140–170 cm
- **Preferred biomes:** Savannas, plains, forests.

Mysterious nomads of the savannas, the Xeroba possess a feline appearance that makes them unmistakable: short fur,
slit-pupil eyes, mobile ears and a prehensile tail. Their culture is deeply tied to ancestral spirits and wild land,
with oral traditions passed down for millennia.

**Distribution:** Nomadic communities in the great savannas and grasslands, especially in subtropical regions. They
rarely settle in cities, but Xeroba caravans cross the kingdoms as itinerant merchants and guides. Their knowledge of
the terrain is renowned.

**Biological traits:** Life expectancy: 60–80 years. Lightning reflexes, perfect balance, excellent night vision.
Directional hearing (mobile ears). Agility superior to any other race, but lower endurance under prolonged effort than
humans and orcs. Prehensile tail used as a third point of support.
