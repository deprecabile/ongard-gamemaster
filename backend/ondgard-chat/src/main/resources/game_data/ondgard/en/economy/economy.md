# ECONOMIC SYSTEM OF ONDGARD

## Currency

Ondgard's monetary system is based on a single coin, the **Denante**, minted by the Ostrian Mint. All kingdoms use the same currency.

### Purchasing Power

Purchasing power is **fixed** and not subject to narrative inflation. The real-world reference is:

- **1 Denante** ≈ 1 euro

### Reference Price List

- Apple, nail, candle: ~1 Denante
- Loaf of bread: ~1 Denante
- Mug of ale: ~1 Denante
- Simple tavern meal: 20–30 Denante
- Full meal with drink: 40–60 Denante
- Tavern night (common bed): 30–50 Denante
- Tavern night (private room): 80–150 Denante
- Dagger, knife: 200–400 Denante
- Bronze sword: ~1,500 Denante
- Light armour (leather): 800–1,500 Denante
- Pack horse: 2,000–4,000 Denante
- Iron sword: 3,000–5,000 Denante
- Trained war horse: 3,000–8,000 Denante
- Full iron/steel armour: 5,000–15,000 Denante

- **Survival reference:** 100 Denante barely cover 2 days of travel (one full meal, one night at an inn, one light meal)

## Economic Rules

- Prices must respect the defined purchasing power. **Inventing out-of-scale prices is forbidden** (e.g. an apple for
  100 Denante or a sword for 10 Denante).
- Transactions must be consistent with the character's available Denante.
- The local economy may vary in goods availability (wartime scarcity, seasonal abundance), but the intrinsic value of
  the currency remains unchanged.
