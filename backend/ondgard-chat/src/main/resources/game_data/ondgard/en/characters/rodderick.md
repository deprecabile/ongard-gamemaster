# Rodderick — Commander of the Northern March

**Title:** Military Commander of the Northern March of Thurim
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 42 years
**Seat:** Nordwacht, Northern March

---

## Appearance

Tall, broad-shouldered, a face that looks carved from the granite of the North. Rodderick wears his hair long in the
manner of Northern warriors and field armour even when other Lords dress in ceremonial attire — a deliberate statement.
Every scar is displayed like a title of nobility.

## Political Profile

Rodderick is the purest product of Thurim's North: martial, pragmatic to the point of brutality, deeply contemptuous
of the "soft Southern Lords" who rule the kingdom from their savanna palaces.

He has risen through the military ranks on genuine competence — his campaigns against raiders along the north-eastern
frontier have earned him unanimous respect among the soldiery. But Rodderick's ambition goes beyond defence: he sees in
the growing rift between North and South of Thurim the **opportunity of a lifetime**.

Should the tension erupt into open crisis, Rodderick is ready to position himself as **leader of the North** — the iron
fist that Northern warriors call for against King Ewald's diplomatic weakness. He is no idealist: he does not fight for
the North's honour; he fights for power. Honour is the language he uses to motivate the soldiers.

## Public Reputation

In the North he is a **hero**: the commander who holds the frontier while the Southern King sells out the kingdom. In
the South he is seen as a dangerous extremist, a man who plays with the fire of civil war for personal ambition. The
truth lies closer to the second description than his followers would care to admit.

## Notable Traits

* Charismatic among soldiers, contemptuous toward nobles
* Cynical and ruthless — loyalty is a tool, not a value
* Shrewd at reading political situations and positioning himself at the right moment
* Genuinely hates Ostria's influence over Thurim and everything the South represents
