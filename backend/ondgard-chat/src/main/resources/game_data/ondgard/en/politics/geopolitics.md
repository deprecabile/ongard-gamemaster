# The Geopolitical Chessboard of Ondgard

The world is not at peace; it is in an armed stalemate. Three superpowers divide global influence in a complex game
where open war is too costly and total peace is impossible. True power does not reside solely in armies but in the
control of debt, of routes safe from Shinosa's tides, and of primary resources.

---

## ⚔️ The Eastern Cold War: The Empire and The Shield

The focal point of global tension lies on the **Eastern Continent**, where two giants face each other along a
fortified land border that has bled for decades without ever fully breaking.

### ⚜️ Empire of Voth (The Sick Giant)

* **Philosophy:** "Order through Submission."
* **The Situation:** Voth is a colossus with feet of clay. It commands the largest army in the world and the most
  fertile lands, yet it is suffocating under the weight of its own bureaucracy and corruption.
* **Strategic Objective:** Voth considers Thurim a rebel province inhabited by barbarians occupying lands that belong
  to the Empire by divine right.
* **Weakness:** Inflation. The cost of maintaining the legions and the imperial apparatus is unsustainable. Voth is
  forced to buy social peace by importing luxury goods, accruing heavy debt with Ostria.
* **The Imperial Senate:** The Emperor does not rule alone. A Senate of patricians, generals and provincial governors
  holds legislative and administrative power. In theory the Senate advises; in practice its internal factions often
  dictate imperial policy, delaying or blocking the Emperor's decisions through cross-vetoes and endemic corruption.
* **The Wall:** The border between Voth and Thurim is not a simple line on the map. It is a fortification system
  stretching kilometres deep — watchtowers, ditches, palisades, barracks and fortress-cities — running for hundreds of
  kilometres along the land frontier. The Empire's finest legions have been pinned here for decades, making any
  strategic redeployment impossible without exposing the northern flank.

### ❄️ Kingdom of Thurim (The Fortress of the North)

* **Philosophy:** "Freedom is paid for in Blood."
* **The Situation:** A nation of brutal contrasts.
    * The **South** (Savannas) is the breadbasket — wealthy and aristocratic, pushing for diplomacy to protect its
      trade.
    * The **North** (Taiga/Tundra) is poor, martial and unyielding, supplying excellent soldiers.
* **Strategic Objective:** Survival. They know a full-scale Voth invasion would be devastating. Their strategy is
  "Active Deterrence": making every metre of ground so costly in lives as to discourage the Empire.
* **Weakness:** The internal divide. The Southern Lords grow increasingly resentful of the troop and tax demands from
  the Northern warriors.

## ⚓ The League of Ostria: The World's Banker

While the East sharpens its swords, the West counts its coins. The **League of Ostria** understood centuries ago that
territorial conquest is a losing proposition.

* **Global Role:** **Armed Neutral**. Ostria has no allies — it has clients.
* **Government:** Ostria is an **oligarchy**. Power rests with the **Council of Seven** — seven sages elected through a
  hierarchical system: eligible voters (notable figures and landowners) and the merchant Guilds select candidates in an
  opaque process of cross-nominations. The Council decides foreign policy, interest rates and declarations of
  neutrality. There is no single sovereign: power is shared, and decisions require a majority of five out of seven.
* **The Debt Strategy:** The League finances both eastern contenders.
    * Lends gold to Voth to pay soldiers and import grain.
    * Buys raw iron from Thurim's mines and sells them **technological manufactures** (gnomish optics, precision
      mechanisms), ships and luxury goods for the Southern nobles.
    * **The goal is not the victory of either side, but the stalemate.** An infinite, low-intensity war is the most
      profitable scenario: it maximises arms sales and minimises the risk that a single victor might then threaten
      Ostria.
* **The Tide Monopoly:** Thanks to gnomish technology and maritime expertise, Ostria is the only power capable of
  navigating safely during the "Shinosa Surges". This effectively guarantees a monopoly on all intercontinental trade.

---

## 👁️ The Dominion of Thia: The Vulture

To the East, silent and menacing, sits the third player.

* **Nature:** A rigid, martial feudal system of orcish tribes, unified under an iron Khanate. They are not
  disorganised savages but a disciplined war machine.
* **Strategic Position:** Borders both Voth and Thurim's sphere of influence (separated by the "Cobalt Sea").
* **The Doctrine of Opportunism:** Thia has no interest in starting a war it might lose. It waits patiently for Voth
  and Thurim to wear each other down. If Voth were to collapse from civil war or bankruptcy, Thia would invade the
  imperial territories that very day to gain access to the Mediterranean Sea. If Thurim were to weaken, Thia would
  seize the southern plains.
* **The Internal Fracture:** The Dominion is ruled by the **Warlords** — the Noyan — who control armies, lands and
  trade. The **priestly caste of Kael**, once influential, has been progressively marginalised and reduced to a
  ceremonial function. The priests officiate rites, interpret omens and bless military campaigns, but real power lies
  with the generals. This humiliation fuels a deep resentment: the priests aspire to restore a theocracy in which the
  word of the clergy carries the force of law, and they await the right moment to eliminate their Noyan rivals — even at
  the cost of allying with external enemies.

## 🌍 The Southern Front: The Second Chessboard

While the world watches the North, a storm brews in the South that only Voth seems to perceive.

### ☀️ The Audrock Threat

For centuries the southern continent was a patchwork of irrelevant tribes and city-states. Now Audrock has changed the
rules: a unified, industrialised kingdom expanding aggressively northward.

* **The Danger:** If Audrock crosses the central mountain ranges and reaches the inner sea, within twenty years it will
  be at Voth's border. The Empire knows it must stop them *now*, while they are still distant and manageable.
* **The Paradox:** With its finest legions pinned at the Wall, every resource sent south is a weakness in the north.
  Voth is forced to choose which front to protect — and right now it lacks the resources for both.
* **Ostria's Role:** The League sees Audrock as a **virgin market** of colossal proportions. Ostrian bankers have
  already established maritime contacts and will do everything to prevent Voth from fragmenting their most promising new
  client. This puts Ostria on a financial collision course with the Empire.

### 🏰 The Buffer State: Dograven

Wedged between Voth and Audrock, the Duchy of Dograven is the first territory that would suffer the consequences of a
southern conflict. Too small to defend itself, too strategic to be ignored — Dograven survives by playing the two
powers against each other, but the room for manoeuvre shrinks every year.

## Global Dynamics

### 1. The "Tide Curtain"

The unpredictable tides caused by the moon Shinosa make navigation in the ocean or the great central sea a mortal
gamble for anyone without Ostria's nautical charts and chronometers.

* **Geopolitical Effect:** No army can invade the continent of Ostria by sea without risking catastrophic losses from
  rogue waves. The sea is the moat of Ostria's castle.

### 2. Monetary Hegemony (The Gold Pact)

The Big Three (Voth, Thurim and Ostria) signed decades ago a binding trade agreement: the only currency accepted for
international exchanges is the **Ostrian Mint**.

* **Geopolitical Effect:** Minor kingdoms, duchies and city-states have had to comply or be excluded from the global
  market. In practice, Ostria controls the inflation and purchasing power of the entire planet. If Ostria decides to
  devalue the currency, Voth's imperial treasury loses value overnight.

### 3. The Silent Front

The real war is fought in teahouses and brothels, not on battlefields. Ostrian spies whisper in the ears of Voth's
generals, while agents of the Dominion of Thia sabotage Thurim's grain stores. The world is a powder keg, and everyone
holds a match.
