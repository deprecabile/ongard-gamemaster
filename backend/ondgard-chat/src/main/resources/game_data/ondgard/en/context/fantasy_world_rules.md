# FUNDAMENTAL RULES OF THE WORLD OF ONDGARD

Ondgard is a **Low Realistic Fantasy** setting. The principles described in this document are inviolable mechanical laws
of the world. They apply without exception.

The only planet known to its inhabitants is **Ondgard**. No other worlds, planets or realities exist within their
cultural and scientific horizon.

> **IMPORTANT — Scope of this document:**
> This document defines exclusively the universal mechanical rules (economy, technology, medicine, magic).
> It does NOT cover specific details of: astronomy, geography, fauna, characters, politics, races, religion.
> If the draft describes elements from those domains, do not judge them — specialised reviewers exist for each.
> Flag a violation ONLY if a rule explicitly listed below is broken.

---

## ECONOMY AND CURRENCY

The world of Ondgard has a single-currency monetary system — the **Denante** — with fixed purchasing power. Minted by the Ostrian Mint, it is the only legal tender across all kingdoms of the globe.

## SCIENCE, TECHNOLOGY AND MEDICINE

### Technological Level

The world is anchored to a **Medieval or Ancient** era.

- **Disparity among civilisations:** Some societies are more advanced (Imperial Rome or early Renaissance level for
  architecture and metallurgy) while others are more primitive (tribal, Bronze Age), but all obey the same physical
  limits.
- **Non-existent technologies:** Firearms (rifles, pistols), electricity and steam engines do not exist. No exceptions.
- **Black powder:** It exists but is rare and unstable. Used exclusively for crude explosive devices (bombs, mines) or
  fireworks. It is not used as a propellant — cannons, arquebuses and muskets do not exist.
- **Naval combat:** Ships fight through ramming, boarding and mechanical siege weapons (ballistae, scorpions, catapults,
  Greek fire).

### Alchemy

Rudimentary chemistry: produces poisons, acids, metal alloys. No instant-effect magical potions exist.

- **Limited knowledge:** The inhabitants of Ondgard do not possess a periodic table of elements nor do they know most
  modern chemical elements. Noble gases (krypton, neon, argon, etc.), radioactive elements and complex molecules are
  entirely unknown to them. Their knowledge is limited to common metals (iron, copper, tin, gold, silver, lead,
  platinum), sulphur, charcoal, precious stones and a few other empirically observable substances.

### Biology and Medicine

- There are no "Hit Points". Damage is localised: fractures, haemorrhages, specific perforations.
- No healing potions or instant magical cures exist.
- Treatments are exclusively medical: suturing, cauterisation, rest.
- Infections and diseases are common and often lethal. Recovery takes weeks or months.

## MAGIC (PHYSICAL SYSTEM)

Magic is a branch of physics based on the conservation and conversion of energy. It is extremely rare and dangerous.

### Source: the Dimensional Rifts

Magic is only possible in proximity to **Dimensional Rifts** — temporary fractures toward the "Zero Dimension", a plane
of pure energy.

### Availability (Meteo-Magic)

Dimensional Rifts follow chaotic meteorological patterns. They vary in frequency, size and duration depending on the
area and time of year. They are predictable only on a large scale.

### Mechanics (Transduction)

The mage is a converter. They channel the energy of a Rift to transform one real energy into another (e.g.
enthalpy → kinetic energy; light → heat).

### Fundamental Constraints of Magic

1. **Proximity:** Physical closeness to an active Dimensional Rift.
2. **Law of Exchange:** Output energy ≤ input energy (minus the mage's efficiency loss).
3. **Ability:** Determines the conversion rate, range and maximum amount of energy that can be manipulated.

### Backlash

- **Failure:** An error in flow control (even from distraction) causes combustion of the mage's biological tissues.
- **Severity of injury:** Proportional to the energy being manipulated.
- **Injury location:** Burns strike body areas in a **completely random** fashion (internal organs, back, face) — not
  necessarily the hands.

### Magical Learning

Steep learning curve. Years of study for modest results. True masters exist but are rare.

## GEOGRAPHICAL SCALE (travel rules only)

- Distances are a primary obstacle. Crossing a kingdom on foot takes **months**, not days. On horseback it takes weeks
  and changes of mount.
- News travels slowly. "Fast travel" does not exist.
- Landscape changes are slow and gradual. No sharp boundaries exist between biomes.
