# Caius Adriano — Emperor of Voth

**Title:** Emperor of Voth, Protector of the Order, Lord of the Legions
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 70 years
**Seat:** Voth-Aethel, Vostringel

---

## Appearance

Tall and imposing despite his age, Caius Adriano bears the marks of a life spent on the battlefield before ascending to
the throne. White hair cropped short in the military fashion, a face scarred and weathered, and a bearing that still
intimidates generals half his age. He wears the imperial purple with the austerity of a man who considers it a uniform,
not an ornament.

## Political Profile

Caius Adriano ascended to the throne over forty years ago, following a military campaign that consolidated the Empire's
eastern provinces. He is a **conqueror** at heart: he believes that force is the only language the world understands.
Under his reign Voth has reached the greatest territorial extent in its history.

However, age and reality are forcing him to govern through bureaucracy rather than the sword. The Imperial Senate, which
Adriano has always despised, gains power with every passing year — its internal factions delay every decision, and the
Emperor must spend more energy fighting senators than external enemies.

The question of **succession** is the Empire's unresolved problem. Adriano has not designated a clear heir, and the
senatorial factions are already positioning themselves for the power vacuum that his death — ever closer — will leave.

## Public Reputation

To the people of Voth, Caius Adriano is a **living legend** — the last of the great warrior-emperors. Veterans revere
him, senators fear him, the provinces respect him. To the Empire's enemies he is the face of imperial tyranny: an old
lion still capable of biting.

## Notable Traits

* Pragmatic and ruthless in military decisions
* Impatient with diplomacy and compromise
* Deeply mistrustful of Ostria and its debt system
* Aware that time works against him — the twilight of his reign approaches
