# King Ewald of Thurim

**Title:** King of Thurim, Warden of the North
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 55 years
**Seat:** Thurigard, Kornthor

---

## Appearance

Of average height and lean build, Ewald has nothing of the monumental warrior the North reveres. He keeps his beard
neatly trimmed in the Southern fashion, wears fine clothes imported from Ostria and prefers wine to ale — details the
Northern Lords have never stopped noticing.

## Political Profile

Ewald is a **man of the South**: born in the fertile savanna plains, raised among the aristocratic Lords who control
Thurim's breadbasket. His ascent to the throne was backed by the Southern nobility, who saw in him a king capable of
prioritising trade over war.

His policy is geared toward **diplomatic stability**: avoiding provocations with Voth, keeping trade routes to Ostria
open, containing military expenditure. For the Southern Lords this is enlightened pragmatism. For the Northern warriors
it is **cowardice**.

The divide is deep. Northern commanders obey the King's orders with growing reluctance, and the Northern clans see in
Ewald a sovereign who has sold Thurim's honour for a few crates of Ostrian wine. The King is aware of the rift but is
convinced that diplomacy is the only way to prevent the kingdom's destruction — and that the North does not understand
how close Thurim is to economic collapse without Southern trade.

## Public Reputation

In the South he is respected as a wise king. In the North he is tolerated — barely. His name is often accompanied by
the contemptuous epithet **"the Merchant King"**, while his supporters call him **"the Prudent"**.

## Notable Traits

* Skilled diplomat who avoids direct confrontation
* Maintains privileged trade relations with Ostria
* Aware of the North-South fracture but unable to heal it
* Fears a Voth invasion more than he fears his own rebel Lords
