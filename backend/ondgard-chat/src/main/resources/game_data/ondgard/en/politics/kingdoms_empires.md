# Kingdoms and Empires of Ondgard

Below are the main kingdoms, empires and nations identified on the planet's political map.

## Western Continent

### ⚓ **League of Ostria**

The largest political entity of the western continent, occupying most of the central and western lands of the
landmass.

* **Geography:** Archipelago and western continent. Temperate rainforests and jagged coastlines. Extent: lat. 24° N – 55° S, long. 80° W – 14° W.
* **Society:** Guild-dominated republic. Predominantly populated by **Humans**, with a strong presence of **Elves**
  and **Gnomes**. Profit-oriented mentality.
* **Economy:** Naval monopoly and banking control. They lend gold to the other powers.

## Eastern Continent

### ⚜️ **Empire of Voth**

A vast empire occupying the heart of the eastern continent, distinguished by its great expanse.

* **Geography:** Occupies a massive peninsula and a vast fertile hinterland. Extent: lat. 56° N – 16° S, long. 20° W – 100° E.
    * *Southern Coast:* Tropical climate, jungles and swamps.
    * *Hinterland:* Vast temperate broadleaf forests and plains.
    * *Northern Border:* Temperate rainforest.
* **Society:** War-based human empire. Advanced in engineering (roads and aqueducts), but in decline.
* **Economy:** High inflation (elevated prices).
*   > **⏳ Time Factor:** Crossing Voth from north to south takes approximately **4–5 months** on well-maintained roads.

### ❄️ **Kingdom of Thurim**

A large nation situated on the north-eastern peninsula of the continent.

* **Geography:** Borders Voth to the south, the sea to the east and north. Separated from the lands of Thia by sea to
  the south-east. Extent: lat. 84° N – 10° N, long. 66° E – 124° E.
    * *South:* Savanna, great plains and temperate broadleaf forests.
    * *Centre:* Temperate broadleaf forests, metal-rich mountains and taiga.
    * *North:* Taiga, tundra and fjords.
* **Society:** Martial and pragmatic. Coexistence of Humans, Dwarves and Orcs for survival.
* **Climate:** In the North, winters are brutal and isolate villages for 4–5 months. In the rest of the kingdom,
  winters are mild and summers warm.
*   > **⏳ Time Factor:** Winter travel in the North is nearly impossible.

### 👁️ **Dominion of Thia**

A vast orcish khanate occupying the wide eastern region of the continent. Not a disorganised horde, but a disciplined
feudal war machine under a single Grand Khan.

* **Geography:** Endless steppes, arid highlands and boreal forests to the north. Borders Voth to the west and
  Thurim's sphere of influence to the north (separated by the Cobalt Sea). Extent: lat. 50° N – 4° S, long. 105° E – 165° E.
* **Society:** Martial feudalism of orcish and goblin tribes, unified by the Khanate. The hierarchy is rigid: the Grand
  Khan at the apex, below him the **Noyan** (Warlords) who control armies and territories, and the **priestly caste of
  Kael** which holds an officially ceremonial role but wields growing influence.
* **Economy:** Pastoral and bellicose. The steppes produce horses and warriors; war spoils and tribute from subjugated
  territories fuel the economy. Limited trade, mainly with Ostria by sea.
* **Military Strength:** Light cavalry superior to any in the world — fast, disciplined, ruthless. Thia's armies move
  at the speed of a galloping herd, making their manoeuvres impossible to predict.

### ☀️ **Kingdom of Audrock**

A geopolitical anomaly: in just a few decades it has gone from a patchwork of tribes and city-states to a unified,
industrialised kingdom expanding aggressively northward.

* **Geography:** Occupies a vast swathe of the southern eastern continent. Extent: lat. 26° S – 64° S, long. 38° E – 73° E. The terrain is
  predominantly flat, with few hills and many navigable rivers. Savannas and temperate deciduous forests to the north,
  great alluvial plains in the centre, cooler grasslands and scrublands to the south.
* **Society:** Absolute monarchy with a brutally hierarchical structure. The conquered have two options: submission or
  slavery. There is no middle ground. Absorbed tribes, city-states and minor duchies lose all autonomy.
* **Economy:** Founded on **systematic slavery**. Forced labour feeds the bauxite and diamond mines, the sandalwood
  deforestation camps and the great infrastructure projects. This model generates rapid economic growth but creates a
  deeply unstable society, dependent on a constant flow of new territories to exploit.
* **Expansion:** Lines of advance point northward, toward the central mountain ranges that separate the South from the
  inner sea. Every year Audrock absorbs new territories, building roads and watchtowers ever further north.
* **Foreign Relations:** The League of Ostria has already established maritime trade contacts with Audrock — for the
  Ostrian bankers the South is not an enemy but a **virgin market** of colossal proportions. Voth watches with mounting
  horror.

### 🏰 **Duchy of Dograven**

A buffer state trapped between two powers that dwarf it: the Empire of Voth to the north and the Kingdom of Audrock to
the south. Its existence is a precarious balancing act.

* **Geography:** Occupies a band of territory between the Empire's southern reaches and Audrock's northern vanguard.
  It is washed to the north by the Inner Mediterranean Sea. Extent: lat. 10° S – 26° S, long. 36° E – 50° E. Highlands and river valleys — fertile land but exposed on both fronts.
* **Society:** A feudal duchy of mixed tradition — Voth's cultural influence is strong (the nobility speaks the imperial
  language, names follow the classical tradition), but proximity to the South has fostered an independent merchant class
  that trades with both sides.
* **Economy:** A trade crossroads between North and South. Caravans linking imperial markets to southern resources pass
  through Dograven, generating wealth but also dependence: if either neighbour closes its borders, the Duchy suffocates.
* **Political Position:** The Duke is **intimidated by both**. Voth treats Dograven as a de facto vassal, demanding
  tribute and troop passage. Audrock sees it as the next territory to absorb. Dograven survives by playing one against
  the other, but the room for manoeuvre shrinks every year.
* **Strategic Value:** Whoever controls Dograven controls the trade routes and mountain passes between the northern and
  southern continents. For Voth it is a defensive buffer. For Audrock it is the gateway to the inner sea.

### 🔱 **Linderion Triumvirate**

Occupies the southernmost tip of the continent. Extent: lat. 56° S – 86° S, long. 31° E – 68° E.

The Triumvirate is one of the most enigmatic political entities on Ondgard: a theocratic oligarchy in which power is formally divided between three Voices — but in practice concentrated in the hands of the **Caste of Fangs**, the sacerdotal-warrior élite of the Xeroba who have governed the territory for generations.

* **Geography:** The Linderian lands are a mosaic of cold grasslands, windswept plateaus and dense boreal forests. Along the southern coasts, sheltered bays provide natural harbours — the only commercial access to an otherwise closed territory. The polar winter lasts six months and isolates large areas from the outside world.
* **Society:** The **Caste of Fangs** is composed of elderly Xeroba initiated into a secret cult of the Earth Spirits. The three members of the triumvirate — the *Three Voices* — are elected by the Caste from among its own elders and govern for life. Decisions require unanimity, making the government slow but unassailable. The population is mixed, with a strong **Dwarf** component concentrated in the mining towns of the highlands.
* **The Dwarven Guilds:** The dwarves of Linderion abandoned the traditional clan structures of Thurim long ago, organising instead into autonomous **Trade Guilds** recognised by the State as separate legal bodies. The most powerful are the *Guild of the Black Iron* (mining and metallurgy), the *Guild of the Eternal Bridges* (civil engineering and architecture) and the *Guild of the Silent Runes* (engraving and precision instrument making). These Guilds pay direct taxes to the Triumvirate in exchange for internal judicial autonomy and exclusive rights to exploit the mineral deposits.
* **Economy:** Based on the extraction of rare metals from the highlands and coastal maritime trade. The Triumvirate exports ingots and dwarven manufactures to the central continent via merchant fleets controlled by the Guilds. For perishable goods — cereals, meat, legumes, fresh medicines — it depends almost entirely on **Audrock**, the only neighbour reachable within a viable timeframe. All other nations lie months of sailing away: more than enough time for any food cargo to spoil. This structural dependency is the Triumvirate’s greatest weakness, and Audrock knows it — using it as diplomatic leverage.
* **Foreign Policy:** The Triumvirate considers itself superior to other nations and refuses permanent foreign ambassadors — accepting only «tribute delegations», even while commercial partners pretend these are normal diplomatic exchanges. The silent rivalry with Audrock along the northern border is tempered by necessity: however much the Triumvirate despises its slaveholding neighbour, it cannot afford to break off trade relations without risking famine.
