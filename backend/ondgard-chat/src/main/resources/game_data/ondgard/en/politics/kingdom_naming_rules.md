# Kingdom Naming Rules

## ⚜️ Empire of Voth

**Inspiration:** Classical World (Ancient Rome and Ancient Greece).
**Vibe:** Imperial, Ancient, Decadent, Martial for the military, Philosophical for mages. Evokes order, law and a
millennia-old history.

* **Male:** Valerius, Aetius, Cassius, Lucius, Keranos, Antiphon, Astraeus, Lysander.
* **Female:** Valeria, Helena, Livia, Cassia, Drusilla.
* **Notes:** Noble names often end in -us, -os, -as.

---

## ❄️ Kingdom of Thurim

**Inspiration:** Germanic, Norse and Norman World.
**Vibe:** Hard as steel, Pragmatic, Feudal. Evokes cold, physical strength and tribal or chivalric loyalty.

* **Male:** Hagen, Tancred, Sigurd, Albrecht, Ewald, Wolfram, Gunther.
* **Female:** Brunhilde, Sigrid, Isolde, Kriemhild, Gudrun.
* **Notes:** Hard sounds, many consonants. No Latinisms.

---

## ☀️ Kingdom of Audrock

**Inspiration:** Punic, Phoenician and Carthaginian Civilisation.
**Vibe:** Exotic (but not "orientalist" in the classical sense), Mercantile, Brutal, Archaic. Evokes ancient deities
and ruthless commercial powers. Sharp contrast with the "western" style of the other kingdoms.

* **Male:** Hannon, Himilco, Barca, Mago, Adherbal, Bomilcar, Hasdrubal, Gisco.
* **Female:** Elissa, Sophonisba, Tanit, Jezebel, Arishat.
* **Notes:** Use of ancient Semitic roots. Names are often short and clipped, or theophoric compounds (incorporating
  names of gods such as Baal or Melqart, adapted).

---

## ⚓ League of Ostria

**Inspiration:** Italian Maritime Republics / Renaissance.
**Vibe:** Mercantile, Refined, Artistic yet Cunning. Evokes banks, exploration, art and complex diplomacy.

* **Male:** Lorenzo, Marco, Vasco, Niccolò, Giovanni, Amerigo, Dante, Cosimo.
* **Female:** Isabella, Lucrezia, Caterina, Beatrice, Sofia, Bianca.
* **Notes:** Surnames often linked to trades, cities or noble houses (e.g. Da Vinci, De Medici, Polo). Frequent use
  of Italian titles such as "Messere" or "Doge".

---

## 👁️ Dominion of Thia

**Inspiration:** Mongol Empire / Golden Horde / Huns.
**Vibe:** Nomadic, Brutal yet Disciplined, Shamanistic. Evokes boundless steppes, speed, hierarchies of strength and
tribal mysticism.

* **Male:** Subutai, Batu, Jebe, Temujin, Oghul, Chagatai, Kael (widespread religious name), Arislan.
* **Female:** Khutulun, Borte, Sorgha, Altyn, Mandukhai.
* **Notes:** Abundant use of titles such as "Khan", "Noyan", "Bek". Sounds that alternate guttural harshness and
  vowel fluidity. Names often carry meanings tied to nature (Wolf, Iron, Sky).
