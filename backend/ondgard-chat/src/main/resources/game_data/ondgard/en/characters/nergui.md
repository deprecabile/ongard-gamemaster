# Nergui — Keeper of the Flame of Kael

**Title:** High Priest of Kael, Court Alchemist to the Grand Khan
**Race:** Orc
**Age:** 58 years
**Seat:** Court of the Grand Khan, Dominion of Thia

---

## Appearance

Gaunt for an orc — almost emaciated — Nergui compensates for his lack of physical presence with an aura that unsettles
everyone nearby. Sunken yellow eyes that seem to see through the words of anyone speaking to him. He wears the
sacerdotal vestments of Kael with the calculated elegance of one who knows that garb is a weapon. His hands, stained
by alchemical reagents, never stop moving — a sacred rosary between his fingers, always.

## Political Profile

Nergui is the true power behind Oghul Khan's throne. Officially he interprets divine signs to guide the sovereign; in
reality he orchestrates every significant decision of the Dominion.

His ambition does not stem from gold or territorial conquest, but from a **thirst for theocratic power**. Thia has been
dominated for generations by the Warlords — the Noyan — who have reduced the priestly caste to a decorative function.
Nergui intends to reverse this hierarchy: he wants a Dominion where the word of Kael's clergy carries the force of law,
and generals are instruments of divine will — with himself at the apex.

To achieve this goal, Nergui works on two fronts:

* **Manipulation of the Khan:** He uses faith and omens to control Oghul, progressively isolating him from secular
  advisors and feeding his insecurity. Every decision the Khan makes passes first through Nergui's filter, presented as
  "divine will".
* **Erosion of the Noyan:** He patiently awaits an opportunity — a war, a crisis — that will decimate the rival
  generals, opening the power vacuum that the priestly caste is ready to fill.

## Public Reputation

To the people he is a devout and respected priest, the voice of the gods at the Khan's court. To the Noyan he is a
harmless old cleric — a miscalculation that Nergui cultivates with care. Only a few keen observers suspect the true
extent of his influence.

## Notable Traits

* Acute intelligence and inexhaustible patience
* Master of psychological manipulation — reads people like sacred texts
* Expert alchemist — his knowledge lends him an aura of supernatural mystery
* Sincerely devoted to Kael — his faith is real, even if his use of religion is cynical
