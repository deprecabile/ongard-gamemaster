# Queen Elissa of Audrock

**Title:** Queen of Audrock, Lady of the Southern Lands
**Race:** Human
**Age:** 31 years
**Seat:** Qart-Hadasht, on the river Neverch

---

## Appearance

Elissa carries herself like someone who grew up knowing the throne was her destiny. Olive complexion, sharp features,
dark eyes that never betray emotion. She dresses with the calculated sobriety of one who needs no ornaments to
communicate power — a single gold ring, ceremonial armour worn like a second skin.

## Political Profile

Elissa ascended to the throne at the age of seventeen, after her father's death. In little more than a decade she has
transformed a fragmented kingdom into a power, unifying by force dozens of city-states, tribes and minor duchies across
the southern continent. The first centralised state south of the equatorial jungle bears her mark.

Her method is brutally simple: **submission or annihilation**. Conquered peoples are integrated as subjects if they
accept Audrock's authority. Those who resist are enslaved and sent to the bauxite and diamond mines, the sandalwood
deforestation camps, the great construction works. Slavery is not a side effect of conquest — it is the kingdom's
**economic engine**.

Her expansionist ambitions point northward: the central mountain ranges, then the passes to the inner sea, then —
inevitably — contact with Voth. Elissa knows that a clash with the Empire is a matter of when, not if. Her strategy is
to build an economic and military base strong enough to sustain that confrontation when it comes.

## Public Reputation

In Audrock she is revered as the **founding mother** — the sovereign who gave the people a national identity and a power
to be feared. For subjugated peoples she is a nightmare. In the rest of the world she is a semi-unknown figure: few in
the North know how much the southern kingdom has grown, and those who do — like Voth — prefer not to say it aloud.

## Notable Traits

* Lucid and patient strategist — plans in decades, not months
* Ruthless but not sadistic — cruelty is a tool, not a pleasure
* Deeply pragmatic: judges allies and enemies solely in terms of utility
* Has established secret diplomatic contacts with the League of Ostria to access technology and markets
