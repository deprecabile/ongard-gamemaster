# Noble Ranks of Ondgard

From highest to lowest:

| Rank                       | Role                                                                                                    |
|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Emperor / Empress**      | Sovereign of an empire, a dominion encompassing multiple kingdoms or peoples under a single crown.      |
| **King / Queen**           | Sovereign of a kingdom. Supreme authority over all crown lands.                                         |
| **Prince / Princess**      | Direct heir or member of the royal family.                                                              |
| **Archduke / Archduchess** | Rules a large strategic province on behalf of the crown. Rare, used only in the vastest realms (Voth).  |
| **Duke / Duchess**         | Lord of a duchy, a vast region with military and judicial autonomy.                                     |
| **Marquess / Marchioness** | Rules a march, a border territory with defence responsibilities.                                        |
| **Count / Countess**       | Administers a county — a city and its surrounding lands. The most common rank among the upper nobility. |
| **Viscount / Viscountess** | The count's lieutenant, managing a portion of the county.                                               |
| **Baron / Baroness**       | The lowest noble rank. Holds a minor fief: a village, a tower, an outpost.                              |
