# Oghul Khan — Grand Khan of the Dominion of Thia

**Title:** Grand Khan of the Dominion of Thia, Lord of the Steppes
**Race:** Orc
**Age:** 34 years
**Seat:** Itinerant court (nomadic tradition of the Dominion)

---

## Appearance

Young for a Grand Khan, Oghul is an orc of powerful build but restless gaze. He wears ceremonial armour with the
stiffness of one who has never worn it in a real battle. The veteran Noyan notice that his hands grip the reins too
tightly — the sign of one who rides out of duty, not instinct.

## Political Profile

Oghul became Grand Khan three years ago, following the sudden death of his father — a legendary warrior who kept the
Warlords in check through charisma and fear. The son inherited neither.

Lacking military victories of his own and surrounded by Noyan who consider him inadequate, Oghul has turned to the only
ally who does not judge him by the strength of his arms: the **priestly caste of Kael**. The priests offer him divine
legitimacy — favourable omens, public blessings, interpretations that confirm his every decision as "the will of the
gods". In return, Oghul grants them ever-growing influence over the court.

The result is that the Grand Khan **does not decide**: he reacts. Every strategic choice passes first through the
priests' filter, who shape the reality Oghul perceives. The Khan believes he rules; in practice he is the unwitting
instrument of the priestly caste.

## Public Reputation

Among the people he is viewed with indifference — he has done nothing yet to earn respect or contempt. The Noyan
consider him a placeholder until a true warrior seizes power. The priests call him **"the Chosen of Kael"** and work to
cement his dependence.

## Notable Traits

* Insecure and easily swayed
* Dependent on validation from the priests of Kael
* Not unintelligent, but lacks the experience to tell counsel from manipulation
* Potentially dangerous if someone were to free him from the priestly influence
